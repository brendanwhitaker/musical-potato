The Fragmented Accountability Paradox

Why high constituent engagement still yields institutional inertia.

The Tension

Credit vs. Accountability

Agencies expand jurisdiction when solutions succeed, but duck responsibility when systemic problems cross operational boundaries.

  • Constituent Cost: Important local issues stall for years in multi-agency review.
  • Operational Cost: Volunteers & staff burnout handling generic outreach loops.
The Core Takeaway

Reframing the Solution

Civic technology cannot fix broken institutional incentives with better communication tools alone. We must build shared execution models across agency boundaries.
